Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Vyxo may collect, why it is used, how it may be shared, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: August 3, 2026

1. Overview

Vyxo is a Discord application designed to provide moderation, automation, community, ticket, economy, music, AI, logging, and related server tools. The Vyxo development team is responsible for the processing described in this Policy.

2. Information We May Collect

Discord identifiers

We may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, and message IDs. These identifiers allow Vyxo to provide commands, remember settings, apply permissions, and associate stored data with the correct Discord account or server.

Server settings and configuration

We may store settings selected by server administrators, such as command preferences, language, moderation rules, log channels, ticket settings, welcome settings, permission roles, and enabled features.

Feature-related data

Depending on the features enabled in a server, Vyxo may store moderation records, warnings, cases, ticket information, economy balances, level or experience data, reminders, custom commands, and similar operational data.

Content submitted to Vyxo

Vyxo may process command inputs and content intentionally submitted through enabled features, such as ticket messages, reports, forms, AI prompts, or configuration fields. Vyxo does not need to store every message in a server unless a specific enabled feature requires that content.

Technical and usage information

We may collect command usage, error reports, timestamps, response times, and security logs to operate, protect, troubleshoot, and improve Vyxo.

3. How We Receive Information

Information may be received:

  • from Discord when you or a server interacts with Vyxo;
  • from commands, forms, tickets, dashboards, or settings you submit;
  • automatically through technical logs generated while the service operates;
  • from third-party services used by an optional feature.

4. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • provide and maintain Vyxo's features;
  • save server preferences and user progress;
  • perform moderation, ticketing, logging, automation, and support functions;
  • detect abuse, fraud, security incidents, and violations of our Terms;
  • diagnose errors and improve reliability and performance;
  • respond to support, privacy, or deletion requests;
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

5. Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable, including in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we process information when it is necessary to provide the service, pursue legitimate interests such as security and service improvement, comply with legal obligations, or act with consent where consent is required.

6. How Information May Be Shared

We may share limited information with:

  • Discord: as necessary for the app to operate on Discord;
  • service providers: such as hosting, database, monitoring, and infrastructure providers;
  • optional integrations: when a server administrator enables a feature that depends on an external API or service;
  • authorities or affected parties: when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights, or address security and abuse.

We do not sell personal information and do not share it with advertisers for targeted advertising.

7. Data Retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Vyxo, maintain security, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations. Retention may depend on the feature and server configuration.

Following a verified deletion request or removal of Vyxo from a server, we aim to delete or anonymize associated data within 30 days, unless a longer period is required for security, legal compliance, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or backup integrity.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, loss, or disclosure. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Never send bot tokens, account passwords, authentication codes, or other secrets through Vyxo commands, tickets, or support messages.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection regarding your personal information. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Server administrators may disable features or remove Vyxo from their server. To submit a privacy or deletion request, contact vyxo.contact@gmail.com. We may ask for reasonable verification before acting on a request.

10. Children's Privacy

Vyxo is not intended for anyone who is not permitted to use Discord under Discord's rules or applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

11. International Data Transfers

Discord and our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used for international transfers.

12. Third-Party Services and Links

Vyxo may contain links to or integrate with third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and we are not responsible for services that we do not control.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when Vyxo's features, data practices, legal requirements, or Discord policies change. The latest revision date will be displayed at the top of this page.

14. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or complaints, contact: vyxo.contact@gmail.com.
